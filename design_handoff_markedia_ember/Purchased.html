Problem

Independent repair shops lose work to manufacturer service networks not because they are slower or more expensive, but because a customer cannot find them at the moment the device breaks. The shops have no scheduling surface, no parts visibility, and no way to quote before the device is on the counter.

Solution

A booking layer sold to shops as a subscription, with a consumer-facing directory as the acquisition channel. The wedge is the appointment; the business is the parts supply that the booking data makes predictable enough to pre-position regionally.

Target audience

Owner-operated repair businesses with one to four technicians, and the customers who currently default to the manufacturer because searching is harder than replacing.

Differentiator

Existing directories monetise the consumer; this monetises the shop and gives the consumer a real-time slot. That inversion is what makes the parts data trustworthy.

Skills a builder needs

Field sales into small businesses, scheduling and inventory modelling, and enough local-SEO literacy to make the directory rank in the first six months.
